# P20 preparation packet and rubric

**DEFERRED. No final panel or pre-review reports executed.** This is a prepared
source packet and assessment protocol for later AI simulation. It is not evidence
that professors, a publisher or an institutional council reviewed the study.

## Common packet

The internal allowlist draft contains the unchanged approved report with its
recorded SHA256, current report sources/bibliography, frozen summary tables,
paired-effect figures, P15 scoped provenance, P16/P17 companion findings, P18
seven-paper comparison and24claim map, source URLs, reading boundaries, release
blockers and the P19 safe-release report. Raw datasets, fitted models, third-party
papers and unreviewed full-text captures are excluded. Missing source captures and
papers must be explicitly requested/retrieved lawfully when a claim needs them.

Every packet file is hashed. A later panel must use one frozen manifest, not a
mixture of report versions or subsequent notes. Individual reviewers receive only
the common immutable packet, common criteria and their preassigned emphasis.
Do not give them this task's concern narrative as hints or one another's reports.
The current packet is an **internal factual status dossier**, not yet the final
blind-review packet. A final dispatch packet must separately remove steering
commentary while keeping the same actual evidence/limitations for every reviewer.

## Rubric, no numerical score invented

| Axis | Evidence to assess | Failure boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Originality | Verified closest methods and precise contribution | Configuration differences or absence of a search hit do not establish algorithmic novelty |
| Scientific importance | IDS error trade-off and limitations | Do not equate high benchmark accuracy with field protection |
| Technical soundness | Split, preprocessing/FS, OOF, tuning, frozen TEST and metrics | Do not claim all selection is nested or fix current protocol after TEST |
| Statistical inference | Paired effects, metric averaging, multiplicity, experimental units | Row-bootstrap is not independent training/session replication; non-rejection is not equivalence |
| Reproducibility | Source/config/data/model provenance and environment | Reconstructed artifacts are not original historical binaries |
| Cybersecurity/generalization | Attack-family support, distribution shift, operational scope | No external/time/unknown-attack/live IDS claim without its experiment |
| Readability and scope | Figures/captions/report snapshot/addendum | Build success does not certify print quality or current provenance prose |
| Availability/release | Allowlist, rights decisions, checksums and software inputs | Mechanical package PASS does not authorize public redistribution |

For a later concern record require stable ID, Major/Minor, blocking Yes/No, exact
claim pointer, evidence pointer, why it affects the case and a resolution test.
Only a Major issue may block the central scientific case. Use NOT_ASSESSABLE
where evidence is missing; no invented quota or inflated score. Final permissible
postures include ACCEPTABLE_WITH_LIMITATIONS, MAJOR_REVISION_REQUIRED or NOT_READY,
with scientific sufficiency distinct from administrative release readiness.

## Proposed emphasis briefs, not reviewer identities

1. Study design, data boundaries and ML/ensemble validity.
2. Statistical inference, IDS error/generalization interpretation and evidence.
3. Prior-art positioning, reproducibility, source support and communication.

These are lenses only. If a full panel is authorized after input gates, use three
isolated contexts and freeze each report before post-review synthesis. No reviewer
is given a shared concern ledger. Do not assert mutual blindness in one context.

## New evidence-grounded defense questions

1. Why is N02's random80/20 result not a head-to-head baseline for our official
   split? Show its Methods locator and our frozen split, rather than its score.
2. What prior-art evidence rules out claiming stacking+FS+UNSW itself as novel?
   Distinguish L06/N02 body evidence from N03 abstract-only evidence.
3. What can be said when a paper describes CV but does not establish full
   selector/scaler/resampling nesting in the sections available?
4. Why do the three L12 accuracy values not justify choosing99.99% as the correct
   number? Show pp16–18 and retain scope uncertainty.
5. What exactly became reproducible in P15, and why does page33 need a companion
   status note rather than silent replacement of the approved PDF?
6. Why can a clean review ZIP coexist with three tracked smoke models, and what
   physical-file risk would a later Git-index deletion commit create on pull?
7. Which rights decisions remain before public code/data/model distribution?
8. What additional evidence would be required to call benchmark CI training
   stability, or to call a two-dataset fit study cross-network transfer?
