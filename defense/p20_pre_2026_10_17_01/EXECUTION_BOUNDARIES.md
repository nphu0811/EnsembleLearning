# Execution and interpretation boundaries

P20-PRE was explicitly authorized by the author at checkpoint
`b85f44180e5055b00e286c2464eebfd1b0f1b46d`. It is a new bounded internal review,
not a rerun of completed P15/P16/P18/P19 acceptance checks and not P20 final.

Three child-agent invocations were launched with `fork_turns="none"`.
Canonical task names are `/root/reviewer_a`, `/root/reviewer_b`,
`/root/reviewer_c`. The common packet/rules and all three emphasis briefs were
hashed in `dispatch_setup.json` before dispatch. Each reviewer received only the
common input paths and its own brief, with no parent chat history, other report,
shared concern list or draft synthesis. Emphasis is assigned by the user's task,
not claimed professional identity or publication credentials.

This provides separate AI invocation/context passes. All use this AI service and
share filesystem access. There is no OS-level file isolation, independent model
provider, human reviewer or independently audited blinding mechanism. Procedural
non-access rules are explicit. Completion records and frozen report hashes record
the boundary actually achieved, not stronger independence.

The previous internal P19 ZIP and prior QA conclusions are not dispatch inputs.
The new local-only packet has 80 entries and its manifest is public evidence of
selection/provenance, not a redistributable public package. The approved PDF and
copied source dossier remain Git-ignored. No third-party full text or model binary
is included. Literature facts are paraphrases from earlier source verification,
with reading/access status. No new DOI, metadata, full-text or VOR verification
is claimed in this task. Links are preserved for subsequent lawful verification.

Packet hashes and structured evidence-pointer QA establish integrity and
traceability, not the correctness of every critique or a publication decision.
Any inaccurate or overstated reviewer inference must stay visible in its frozen
report and receive a separate adjudication, not a silent edit after synthesis.

Defense drafting by the parent can run while reviewers assess independently. It
is never shown to reviewers. Final concern mapping and synthesis start only after
the three reports are finalized and frozen. A separate synthesis invocation then
receives the frozen reports and the same packet. No reviewer is asked to conform
its concerns or recommendation to another report.

No raw TEST, row-level predictions, new bootstrap, model loading, training,
threshold/config changes, locked-report/bibliography edits, index cleanup,
LICENSE selection, public internal-ZIP publication or final submission package
is authorized by this pre-review. All frozen-source checks in this task are
checksums/anchors for this new review operation, not repeated model acceptance.
